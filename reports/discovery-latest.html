Site health · Phase 1 audit · Discovery + crawl audit

Two read-only passes (GET only, robots.txt respected, one request per host per second). Discovery read every host's robots.txt and sitemaps. The crawl audit then fetched a sample of pages per template and checked status codes, redirects, indexability, canonicals, hreflang and the main head tags. Speed, structured data and content checks come in later audits.
