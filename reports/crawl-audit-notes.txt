Scope: all 29 products families (run 9, sampled per template and language) plus every other host and all homepages. Families without a working sitemap (omr, drawing) were sampled from their top Search Console pages. Private forum topics (login required) are skipped.
products /cells/: 22 of 26 sampled pages declare a canonical without "/cells" (e.g. /cells/conversion/xls points to https://products.aspose.app/conversion). Every one of those canonical targets returns HTTP 404. Search Console URL Inspection confirms the damage: Google overrides it on strong pages but drops others as "Alternate page with proper canonical tag" (/cells/form/to-do-list/xlsx, /cells/remove-watermark). In May the canonicals were still correct, so this is a recent regression.
products /cad/ and /psd/: translated pages declare the English page as their canonical (e.g. /cad/tr/conversion/fbx-to-3ds points to /cad/conversion/fbx-to-3ds). That tells Google to drop the translations and cancels their hreflang.
products /slides/: all 24,752 URLs in the slides sitemaps are written with a double slash (https://products.aspose.app//slides/...) and return HTTP 503. Search Console only knows the single-slash pages, found through links.
hreflang lists every language except the page itself in 14 families (zip, html, total, 3d, omr, svg, font, note, page, pub, tasks, tex, ocr, words hubs); English pages have only x-default pointing to them. One shared component is the likely cause. barcode app pages have hreflang but no x-default; the family hubs have no hreflang at all.
words hubs (/words/, /words/family/ and their language versions) have no canonical, and the sitemap lists them without the trailing slash, which redirects.
Server errors seen in two runs: some cad hubs (/cad/fr, /cad/it) return HTTP 500, ocr pages 500/502, and drawing pages (/drawing/online-ruler) 503.
releases.aspose.app: release-notes pages are noindex, nofollow but listed in the sitemap, and pages have no canonical, no <html lang> and no meta description.
websites.aspose.app: sampled sitemap URLs include meta-refresh redirect pages (/en/, /fileformat/), and /ko/, /nl/ and other language pages declare <html lang="en">. metrics, www and websites have language versions but no hreflang in the page HTML.
Zoom is disabled (user-scalable=no) on blog, releases, status and websites (accessibility, WCAG 1.4.4). status.aspose.app lists /dashboard in its sitemap, which redirects through three 302s to an SSO login. api.products.aspose.app returns 404 on its homepage.
